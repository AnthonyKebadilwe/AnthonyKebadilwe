## Suggested 16-week schedule

| 1–3 | Python, NumPy/pandas, vectors, probability and AI fundamentals | Clean a dataset and explain basic statistics |

Weekly allocation: approximately four hours of lessons, four of labs, two of recall/timed questions and one documenting progress. Start timed practice and labs early.

Suggested project: a document-question-answering assistant or crop-image classifier. Define a test set and measurable success criteria before implementation. Publish setup instructions, evaluation results and limitations. Do not commit passwords, cloud keys or private datasets.

## Competition analysis

The Cloud learning space explicitly covers Cloud and AI. It is the recommended starting route for an individual applicant interested in AI. Individual registration does not establish that later rounds are solo.

The Southern Africa regional rules displayed:

| Stage | Assessment | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Preliminary | 60 theory questions | 60 minutes |
| National | 150 theory questions | 120 minutes |
| Regional | Scenario-based lab exam | 240 minutes |
| Global | Scenario-based lab exam | 480 minutes |

The regional portal listed 31 January 2027 for Practice registration and 24 December 2026 for Innovation. Botswana-specific deadlines, exam dates, selection requirements and later team formation still need confirmation. The regional deadline is not a guaranteed preparation window. Older papers supplement the current outline.

The separate AI Innovation route involves building and demonstrating an AI application. Its form requests a team name, two teammate accounts and an instructor. Regional project guidance requires at least one Huawei Cloud service; shortlisted regional/global entries must be open-sourced. Travel to China depends on qualification, not registration.

## Professional certification steps

1. Confirm the exact HCIA-AI exam, language and version offered in Botswana.
2. Match training material, exam outline, labs and official practice resources to that version.
3. Complete relevant study and hands-on practice.
4. Ask the institution’s Huawei ICT Academy about vouchers and support.
5. Confirm fees, identity requirements and testing arrangements through Huawei/Pearson VUE before booking.
6. Pass the required assessment and obtain the issued e-certificate.

Course certificates, competition participation and professional certification are separate achievements. Only list HCIA as earned after passing the required assessment and receiving the credential. HCIP and HCIE are later options after practical experience.
